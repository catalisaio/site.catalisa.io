Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: final editorial hero key visual for the new Catalisa website, landscape 3:2 composition, no lettering so the artwork works across languages.
Primary request: a refined physical sculpture that makes independent capabilities visibly connect into one coherent product; replace a generic placeholder purple city illustration with a distinctive, crafted, mature image.
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory paper studio cyclorama, seamless off-white #f7f4ee background that blends into the website. One precise, restrained architectural assemblage, six distinct modular objects at different levels on a thin low plinth, connected by clearly purposeful orange paths that split and recombine. A tactile gallery object, not a toy city.
Style/medium: high-end contemporary industrial design photography, convincing real materials and subtle surface texture, exceptional composition and restraint. Bauhaus balance with a coherent geometric vocabulary, rounded and rectilinear modules, one circular aperture, one thin layered stack, a branching connector. Each piece has an individual function expressed by form. Calm, intelligent, optimistic.

Lighting: soft large studio key light from upper left, subtle contact shadows, realistic ambient occlusion, rich matte surfaces and gentle reflections. Warm paper backdrop stays light.

Constraints: no words, no logo, no UI, no charts, no numbers, no watermarks, no humans or hands. No neon, no glow, no gradients used as effects, no robots, no brains, no crypto/coins, no generic tech stock art. Preserve generous paper-colored negative space. Premium and believable rather than plastic or cartoonish.
